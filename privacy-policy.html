Privacy Policy for NanoMind AI

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

At NanoMind AI, we prioritize your privacy. This application is designed to run AI models entirely on your device.

1. Data Collection

NanoMind AI does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, chat history, prompts, or AI-generated responses to us or to any external server. All AI processing (inference) happens locally on your device's hardware and never leaves it.

2. Internet Access

This app requires internet access solely to download AI model files (from Hugging Face or a URL you provide) so they can be stored and run locally. Once a model is downloaded, no internet connection is required to chat with it, and no conversation data is sent over the network.

3. Model Files and Local Storage

Models you download are stored in this app's private storage on your device. We do not track, log, or transmit which models you download or how you use them.

4. Custom Model Imports

If you choose to import a model from a custom URL, that request goes directly from your device to the address you provide (which may not be under our control). We do not inspect, log, or store the contents of custom downloads. You are responsible for ensuring any custom source is trustworthy and that you have the right to download and use the file.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet: To download model files.
  • Notifications: To show download progress and completion status.
  • Background/Foreground Service: To allow large model downloads to continue and survive the app being backgrounded.

No permission is used to access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

6. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in this application.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect any data from children, since we do not collect personal data from any user.

8. Third-Party Model Providers

Model files are hosted by third parties (e.g., Hugging Face, or a source you provide). Downloading a model means your device connects directly to that host; please review their respective privacy practices as well.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
