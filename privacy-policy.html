Privacy Policy for NanoMind AI

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

At NanoMind AI, we prioritize your privacy. This application is designed to run AI models entirely on your device.

1. Data Collection

Chat history, imported documents, extracted passages, and document-grounded answer references are stored locally in the app's private storage. All AI processing (inference) happens locally on your device. The app does not send conversations or documents to us unless you choose to include information in a report.

2. Internet Access

Internet access is used for model downloads, loading fonts from Google when needed, app update checks, and other explicitly networked app features. These services may receive connection information such as your IP address.

Document import, indexing, retrieval, and document-grounded chat do not require a network connection once the chat model is on the device. No conversation or document data is sent over the network by the document retrieval feature.

3. Model Files and Local Storage

Models, documents you select, extracted passages, and their local search index are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can remove imported documents in My Documents. Existing chat answers and their source references remain in local conversation history until you delete that conversation.

4. Reports

If you submit a report, the app sends the model name, reason, optional details, optional response excerpt, and submission time to us using Google Cloud Firestore. Google may receive connection information such as your IP address. Please do not include sensitive information.

A report submitted while offline may be sent when the connection returns. We use reports to investigate and improve safety; they remain stored until deleted. Contact us at the address below to request deletion. Reports previously saved only on your device are not automatically uploaded.

5. Custom Model Imports

If you choose to import a model from a custom URL, that request goes directly from your device to the address you provide, which may not be under our control. We do not inspect, log, or store the contents of custom downloads. You are responsible for ensuring any custom source is trustworthy and that you have the right to download and use the file.

6. Permissions Used

  • Internet: To download model files and use the networked features described above.
  • Notifications: To show download progress and completion status.
  • Background/Foreground Service: To allow large model downloads to continue when the app is backgrounded.

The system document picker only provides access to files you choose to import; the app does not request broad access to your storage or photo library. No permission is used to access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

7. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in this application.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, or the applicable age of digital consent in your region, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Third-Party Model Providers

Model files are hosted by third parties, such as Hugging Face or a source you provide. Downloading a model means your device connects directly to that host; please review its privacy practices as well.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact
